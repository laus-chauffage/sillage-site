Everything Sillage does, in the clear

  • One screen to log a flight: your airfields first, then 19,277 ICAO codes; the total follows from block times, across midnight; what is inconsistent is refused and named, what is doubtful is flagged
  • The track recorder keeps going with the screen off, then reads take-offs, landings, touch-and-goes, runway direction, cruise and wind — and proposes the flight, which you accept line by line
  • Replay the flight on the map, and a card to share as a Story, portrait or square — never the remarks, the people, the licence or the signature
  • Side by side: a flight, a signature request, a solo authorisation, one or several aircraft sheets pass from one phone to the other, up close, with no network and no account
  • Your instructor signs on YOUR screen — initials, name, licence, no app — or seals with Face ID if they have Sillage, with a key that never leaves the secure chip
  • Every signed line is sealed: changing a time afterwards breaks verification, and it shows
  • Night time computed for the country of the flight as you log; passenger recency and SEP revalidation gauge on the home screen
  • Licences, class and type ratings, medical, language proficiency: their expiry dates, and a revalidation period proposed from the regulation, never blocking
  • Your aircraft: 48,053 registrations and 2,615 type designators on board, the photo, and the on-board documents with their expiry dates
  • Bring your old logbook: CSV, text or XLSX files exported from the most widely used logbook apps, or printed pages, read on the phone — or simply carry your totals forward
  • Official AMC1 FCL.050 logbook as a sealed PDF, in the language of your licence's country, plus CSV — or “View my logbook” full screen, with no network
  • Statistics and a map of visited airfields: per year, per aircraft, and what your tracks tell

Your data doesn't fly away

  • Your logbook never leaves your phone. No account, no Sillage server: the app sends us nothing, and we have no database to query, lose or sell.
  • If you turn on iCloud backup, it goes to YOUR iCloud, not ours — we have no access to it. Delete the app, reinstall it: the logbook comes back.
  • Your exports are yours: PDF and CSV that open without Sillage, and a complete copy of your logbook to keep wherever you like.
  • Every signed line is sealed inside your phone: changing it afterwards breaks the seal, and it shows.

Designed and published by a working flight instructor. EASA Part-FCL format, AMC1 FCL.050 columns.

A logbook that counts

Electronic logbooks are accepted by the regulation: FCL.050 requires a reliable record, not a particular medium. Columns follow AMC1 FCL.050, sealed PDF, signatures sealed inside your device.
